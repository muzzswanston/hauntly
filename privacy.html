Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 5, 2026

Welcome to Mystery Atlas ("we", "us", or "our"), available at https://mysteryatlas.app. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your data when you interact with our interactive map directory, newsletter, and affiliate partners.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in privacy-first design and minimize data collection whenever possible.

  • Privacy-Friendly Analytics: We use Umami Analytics to understand website traffic. Umami is a privacy-focused analytics engine that does not store persistent tracking cookies, does not collect personal identifiers, and respects visitor anonymity under GDPR and CCPA standards.
  • Geolocation Data: When you use our optional "Near Me" (📍) GPS functionality, your browser requests permission to share your coordinates. This location data is processed locally in your web browser solely to center the map and calculate nearby points of interest. We do not store or transmit your real-time location to external servers.
  • Community Mystery Submissions: If you voluntarily submit a mystery, location report, or story via our submission modal, we collect the details provided (e.g., location name, description, region, coordinates) to review and display publicly. Please do not submit personal or private identity details in public story fields.
  • Newsletter Subscriptions: If you subscribe to our newsletter ("The Mystery Atlas Dispatch"), your email address is processed through our third-party provider (Beehiiv) strictly for delivery of weekly newsletters. You may unsubscribe at any time via the link in the footer of any email.

2. Cookies & Affiliate Tracking Technologies

While Mystery Atlas does not drop invasive third-party profiling cookies on our core directory, we participate in performance affiliate programs (such as CJ Affiliate, Booking.com, and Viator):

  • User-Initiated Tracking: When you click on external booking or tour links (e.g., "Book Hotel Nearby", "Book Ghost Tour"), you leave Mystery Atlas and navigate to external third-party merchant sites.
  • Affiliate Partner Cookies: These third-party affiliate networks may use session cookies, web beacons, or link identifiers to track referrals and reward affiliate commissions for completed purchases. Tracking occurs only after an explicit, user-initiated click on an affiliate link.

3. How We Use Information

We use collected data solely to:

  • Operate and improve the interactive map interface and user navigation.
  • Publish verified user-submitted paranormal and historical entries.
  • Send requested email news and newsletter updates.
  • Maintain compliance with affiliate network disclosure standards and privacy laws.

4. Legal Disclaimer & Third-Party Content

Mystery Atlas operates as an independent factual location directory and digital publication. References to television series, production networks, public figures, or celebrity investigators are used strictly for descriptive and informational purposes under nominative fair use. Mystery Atlas is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or officially associated with any television network or public personality mentioned.

5. Data Sharing & Third-Party Service Providers

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. Data is shared with service providers only as necessary to deliver core features:

  • Database Hosting: Supabase (Cloud database infrastructure).
  • Mapping APIs: Google Maps JavaScript API (Map rendering and geometry).
  • Newsletter Distribution: Beehiiv.
  • Analytics: Umami Analytics (Cookie-less, GDPR-compliant).

6. Your Data Protection Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your region (including the EU, UK, Australia, and California), you hold rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access & Rectification: Right to request a copy of or corrections to data you submitted.
  • Deletion ("Right to be Forgotten"): Right to request removal of your user submissions or newsletter subscription details.
  • Opt-Out of Analytics: You may use standard ad-blocking extensions or browser settings to prevent script execution if desired.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions, comments, or data requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out through our newsletter portal at Mystery Atlas Dispatch.
